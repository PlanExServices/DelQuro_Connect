# Veterinary team context and product naming brief

Research snapshot: October 8, 2026. The local preview still uses **DelQuro Connect** as a working name. No replacement name has been selected.

## The organization and reference hospital

- Southern Veterinary Partners and Mission Veterinary Partners formally merged in late 2024 and announced the combined **Mission Pet Health** brand in July 2025. Mission describes its model as support for local hospital teams with medical autonomy and professional development. [Mission Pet Health announcement](https://missionpethealth.com/2025/07/21/southern-veterinary-partners-and-mission-veterinary-partners-join-together-as-mission-pet-health/)
- The reference Commonwealth Animal Hospital is the one at **4721 Market Square Lane, Midlothian, VA 23112** (`commonwealthanimal.com`). Its public site describes wellness, diagnostics, and surgery. This is a different hospital from similarly named practices in Charlotte, NC and Fairfax, VA. [Commonwealth Animal Hospital](https://www.commonwealthanimal.com/), [About us](https://www.commonwealthanimal.com/about-us/)
- Its public [Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/commonwealthahpets) lists the same Midlothian address and hospital website. The visible posts include pet adoption updates and use a warm community voice. Facebook limits how much of the feed a signed-out visitor can read; this is a signal about public communication, not evidence of internal staff culture.

## What a hospital day asks of the team

This is a composite from published veterinary sources, **not a claim about CAH's internal routines**.

| Moment | Work and coordination pressure |
| --- | --- |
| Opening and first appointments | Client service staff field calls and arrivals; assistants and technicians prepare patients, rooms, and procedures; veterinarians review cases; managers adjust coverage. The AVMA names all of these roles as part of the veterinary team. [AVMA team roles](https://ebusiness.avma.org/ProductCatalog/product.aspx?ID=2315) |
| The middle of the day | Scheduled exams run alongside urgent calls, lab work, procedures, hospitalized patients, refills, callbacks, and client updates. A technician's account of competing demands and AAHA's whole-team communication guidance show why information must move cleanly between roles. [AAHA technician workload](https://www.aaha.org/trends-magazine/october-2024/tech-support/), [AAHA whole-team communication](https://www.aaha.org/trends-magazine/april-2022/cc-communication/) |
| Hard conversations | Staff may support grieving families, deal with client frustration, and face cases where finances limit care. End-of-life decisions and moral distress can affect clinical and client service staff. [AAHA end-of-life team guidance](https://www.aaha.org/resources/2023-aaha-senior-care-guidelines-for-dogs-and-cats/the-veterinary-team-in-end-of-life-care/), [AAHA client service](https://www.aaha.org/trends-magazine/june-2022/f2-csr/), [AAHA financial triage](https://www.aaha.org/trends-magazine/publications/financial-triage-expanding-care-for-pets-their-people-and-your-practice/) |
| Handoffs and close | The next person needs to know what was promised, what is pending, and who is responsible. AAHA describes verbal rounds, alerts, and record notes as ways teams maintain continuity. [AAHA teamwork and disease management](https://www.aaha.org/trends-magazine/november-2023/ht-teamwork-and-disease-management/) |

The work has real rewards as well: better patient outcomes, relationships with families, professional skill, and supportive coworkers. A name should respect that purpose without romanticizing the workload. [AAHA on the joy and work of veterinary medicine](https://www.aaha.org/trends-magazine/publications/view-from-the-board-the-joy-and-journey-of-veterinary-medicine/), [AAHA on sustainable workloads](https://www.aaha.org/trends-magazine/publications/view-from-the-board-building-a-veterinary-workplace-where-people-can-thrive/)

## Implications for this app

The owner also has a public [veterinary workplace survey](https://survey.delqurolabs.app/) that asks people to describe one recent shift moment, its blocker and ripple effect, what they used, and what their team tried. We reviewed the questionnaire without submitting a synthetic response. Its answers, when supplied by real team members, can check which coordination problems merit the most attention.

These are **design inferences**, to validate with the people who will use the app.

1. Make the shared hospital chat useful during a busy shift: clear unread state, concise handoffs, and a separate way to acknowledge important updates. Do not make every routine message feel urgent.
2. Protect off-duty time. The requested on-site alert setting fits that goal; messages can remain readable from anywhere while ordinary push alerts follow each teammate's work status and preferences. Urgent escalation needs an explicit policy rather than an implicit expectation that every teammate is always available.
3. Give front desk, assistants, technicians, veterinarians, and managers equally natural language in the interface. Terms that only clinicians use can make other teammates feel like guests in their own tool.
4. Keep the hospital's name visible as the workspace identity. Choose a product name that can serve another hospital without renaming the software.
5. Use a calm, professional voice. CAH's affectionate public language speaks to pet owners; internal staff software also has to feel credible during difficult cases and shift changes.

A preliminary product scan found existing veterinary team chat in [medikit](https://medikit.net/solutions/veterinary/) and [Weave](https://www.getweave.com/use-cases/veterinary-team-chat/), while many practice systems center on client communication or records. The proposed app's story should be about reliable team coordination and reasonable alert boundaries, not merely the presence of chat. This is a positioning inference, not a complete market survey.

## Naming criteria we can test together

- Easy to say aloud and spell after hearing it once.
- Professional enough for a hospital and its HR/IT review; avoid words that sound romantic, infantilizing, or like a pet-owner app.
- Inclusive of the entire team and broad enough for chat, schedules, time off, and updates.
- Memorable without implying affiliation with a specific prospective customer.
- Distinct from existing veterinary software; pass a proper trademark and domain review before selection. [USPTO search guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/federal-trademark-searching)

**Current candidates and exclusions:** DelQuro Connect remains the preview name but is difficult for new users to spell. VetHuddle is excluded as a **product name** because its sound is HR-rejectable; the **Huddle tab is mandatory** and stays in the app. The earlier suggestion Vet Team Connect is descriptive, but `Connect` is crowded in veterinary software: [Vetigen Connect](https://vetigen.com/products/connect) and [MVH Connect](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mvh.softphone) are already in use. These observations are a preliminary screen, not trademark clearance. No new name is approved yet.

## Questions for short team conversations

1. When you need another teammate during a shift, what do you call that interaction: an update, handoff, message, request, or something else?
2. Which information most often gets lost between front desk, treatment area, and doctors?
3. Which notifications help during a shift, and which feel like noise or follow you home?
4. Would you naturally call your workplace a hospital, clinic, or practice?
5. Which candidate names would you feel comfortable saying to a new teammate or putting on a workstation sign-in screen?

These conversations should complement public research; they are the best way to learn CAH's actual vocabulary without guessing from the website.
